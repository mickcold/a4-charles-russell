How to use this

Each crime from the dataset (up to 350) is turned into a math function and graphed

  • Police force: What UK police force's crime data is being used
  • Crime type: What kind of crime it was
  • Longitude: The longitude of where the crime took place
  • Latitude: The latitude of where the crime took place
  • Hover over a mark for crime details
